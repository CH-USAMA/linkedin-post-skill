# Weekly workflow

## Suggested cadence
| Day | Lane | Default asset |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | News take on your stack (verified, last 7 days) | Image + short reveal video |
| Wednesday | First-person story from your real work | Image (before/after or timeline) |
| Friday | Skimmable practical (checklist, X vs Y) | PDF carousel, 6 to 10 slides |

Never post twice on the same day. Pick posting times in your audience's time zone.

## The loop
1. **Read state**: `history.jsonl`, `ideas.json`, `voice.md`.
2. **Choose the lane** for today and a topic that has not been used.
3. **Research** with ranked sources (official docs and release notes first). Note the URL for the first comment.
4. **Design** the asset. One idea per image or slide.
5. **Write** the caption, then **lint** it until clean.
6. **Save** files and append a `draft` line to `history.jsonl`.
7. **Present** a ready-to-post block: numbered steps, caption, file names, first comment, alt text, time.
8. **You approve** and post. Change the history line to `posted` and, a day later, add the metrics.

## Scheduling drafts
You can run step 1 to 7 on a schedule (for example 8am on posting days) and have the agent notify you. Keep publishing manual. See [safety-and-tos.md](safety-and-tos.md).

## Engagement
Thoughtful comments on larger creators' fresh posts grow reach more than posting more. The skill can draft two comments for you to review and paste yourself. It never comments unattended.

## Measuring
Record impressions, reactions, comments, reposts and saves in `analytics.json`. Review monthly: rank posts by engagement rate and reach multiple (impressions divided by followers), not raw impressions only. Feed the winners back into your lanes.
