# Hook formulas (checklist for line 1; adapted from the 21-formula idea in Jakeschincariol/linkedin-agent-skill, MIT)
Pick ONE per post, name it in the receipt, and check its failure mode. Never invent dialogue, numbers or incidents.
Best fits by lane:
- MONDAY news: Myth Bust (popular explanation is not the real cause, name the real one), The Warning (common practice quietly costs X, give the fix in the same post), Contrarian Take (only against a view people actually hold), The Comparison (don't rig it).
- WEDNESDAY story (story-bank facts only): Story Cold Open (ONLY real dialogue from the bank, else skip), The Receipt (hard figure from the bank + one line of context), Mistake Confession (specific cost tied to ONE error; fails if it reads as a humble-brag), Before/After (ONE causal lever, not several), The Walk-Away.
- FRIDAY practical: The List Promise (max ~10, ideally 5-7), The Callout (a habit people really have), Direct Value (give the artifact in the carousel, don't withhold), Question Trap (realistic scenario, no obvious right answer).
Failure modes to avoid: arguing against a view nobody holds; burying the number; several causes instead of one lever; clickbait gap never paid off; Pattern Interrupt overuse; anxiety with no fix.
Close: one divisive, specific tradeoff question (our rule). Not "Thoughts?" / "Agree?".
