Every endpoint you ship is a promise. Here are 5 checks before you make it.

- Validate the input at the edge
- Set a timeout on every outbound call
- Retry on purpose, not by default
- Log what failed, not what the user typed
- Test the unhappy path once

Do you block a merge for a missing timeout, or just leave a comment?
